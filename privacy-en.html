Kutukac: 3D Arrow Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy applies to Kutukac: 3D Arrow Puzzle (the "App"), developed by Ahmet Gül ("we").

Data we collect

  • The App has no account or login. We do not ask for your name, email or contact details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • To improve the game we collect anonymous gameplay usage data (product interaction): session start and length, level start, completion, time running out, leaving a level, hint use, tutorial step and world, together with time used, stars, mistakes, moves and hints used.
  • This data is sent with a random install ID generated on your device to our analytics provider GameAnalytics. Analytics requests also contain the device type (iPhone), operating system version (e.g. iOS 17.4), app and SDK version and a random session ID. Name, email, location or advertising identifier are not sent.
  • This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Analytics. When off, data waiting on the device is deleted and no analytics data is sent.
  • The App contains no ads and no tracking. It does not access your device advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for tracking permission.
  • App Store privacy label: Usage Data > Product Interaction and Diagnostics > Other Diagnostic Data; not linked to you, not used for tracking, purpose: Analytics.

Level packs and analytics

New levels may be downloaded from the internet; if analytics is on, analytics data is also sent. These are the only network requests the App makes. As with any internet request, servers can technically see your IP address in order to respond; GameAnalytics may derive a coarse country from it. This is not used to identify or track you.

Sale and sharing of data

We do not sell data. Analytics data is processed only by our service provider GameAnalytics on our behalf, to improve the game.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe your child has shared data, please contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, KVKK), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Deleting the App removes all data stored on your device. Contact us with any request.

Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is published on this page.
